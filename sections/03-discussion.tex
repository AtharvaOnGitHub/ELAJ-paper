\section*{Discussion}

\elaj is a transformer-CVAE based disease atlas for endometriosis spanning 
eleven scRNA studies, 1{,}577{,}728 cells and 254 donors. Every donor was 
biopsied once, which created a confound between biology and batch effects 
\citep{hrovatin2025atlases}. In \elaj's sixteen-dimensional latent space,
donor identity is suppressed while cell identity and the menstrual cycle
remain recoverable. Cell identity separates without ever having been
supervised, and the cycle resolves within cell types rather than across the
corpus as a whole.

\elaj\ carries 1{,}883{,}986 parameters against scFoundation's 100 million, 
pretrained on over 50 million profiles \citep{hao2024scfoundation}. Four 
pretrained models conferred no advantage on this corpus over \elaj\
\citep{cui2024scgpt,theodoris2023geneformer,heimberg2025scimilarity,kedzierska2025zeroshot}.
scGPT's own authors note that its pretraining does not itself mitigate 
batch effects \citep{cui2024scgpt}. Its size is what made the trade-off
measurable: eleven configurations were trained and scored on identical cells,
where a representation of fixed pretrained weights yields a single point.

The \elaj frontier, defined on menstrual phase against donor or study identity,
is shaped by adversarial weight and information cost. While adversarial weight 
improves the achievable trade-off up to a critical value and gives worse results 
past it, the information cost slides the model along the trade-off. Integration
methods that expose one correction strength therefore report a position on
this trade-off rather than an optimum
\citep{korsunsky2019harmony,lopez2018scvi,xu2021scanvi,hie2019scanorama}.
None of the nine competing representations reached \elaj's cross-donor cycle
agreement at a lower donor recovery. Cell identity was recovered by every one
of them and separates none (Table~1), so the cycle and donor identity are the
axes on which these representations differ at all
\citep{luecken2022benchmarking}.

Cell composition does not differ between control and eutopic endometrium,
confirming at 194 donors a claim made in 60
\citep{duempelmann2026endometrium}. The claim that endometriosis abolishes
cyclical variation in immune composition failed at 112 and 101 donors against
the 13 that produced it \citep{huang2023immune}. The disease signal that does
survive lies inside immune cells, where decoding it returned macrophage-led
inflammation. Its leading genes overlap an independent cohort's upregulated
list at \textit{P}~=~3.95~\texttimes~10\textsuperscript{--7}
\citep{zhao2017transcriptome}, and two of them are absent from the resources
that index gene-disease associations \citep{cominelli2014mmp27}. Eleven
studies had converged on overlapping findings with no means of confirming one
another, because no two share donors or a cell vocabulary
\citep{mareckova2024heca}. Confirmation, contradiction, and external
replication are three functions a disease atlas is best suited to perform.

rASRM staging does not correspond to anything the latent separates. Whether a
person has endometriosis was recoverable from endometrium inside the uterus.
Severity, a grouping derived from rASRM staging, was legible only when
controls were included. A headcount of cell families did not separate severity
either. Severity divided control from disease and nothing within disease, so
what a surgeon scores is not what the tissue distinguishes.

The main architectural bet made by \elaj is the replacement of scale with
inductive bias. Ablation of pathway groups showed the biological axis
degrading, while menstrual-phase head supervision proved to be a regulariser
over the whole latent. Without the elastic cell similarity head, large
single-study territories appear that \elaj\textsubscript{B\,hi} does not
have. Replacing the dataset-native sentinel with zeros reorganises the latent
the same visible way. Removing the pooled order statistics,
which cuts what each group hands forward from 288 numbers to 32, cost the
menstrual cycle heavily and cell identity almost nothing. A weighted average
discards the shape of a group's distribution, and on this corpus that shape
carries the cycle.

Epithelial pseudotime and serum progesterone were recovered more strongly
than mitochondrial fraction, library size or doublet score, so biology
outranks technical variation across the sixteen dimensions, none of which the model was given.
Tissue of origin, the one label supplied through decoder conditioning, is
largely absent from the latent, which is the conditioning working as designed.
Cell-family labels also transfer across studies accurately enough to annotate
the cohorts that deposited none, which is what brought them into the
composition tests above.

Notably, the negative results reported here are
bounded by donor count rather than by biology, since the width of a
permutation null is set by the number of people contributing to it. Severity
and surgical stage remain claims that a larger corpus could settle. Three
further constraints on this corpus, overlapping donor identifiers across one
study pair, a single cohort recording cycle day, and an unstratified
permutation null, are detailed in Supplementary Note~X.

Transfer beyond endometriosis rests on two conditions rather than on the
disease, scarce donors and severe batch structure, which hold wherever
diagnosis requires surgery. A less differentiated epithelium has been
reported in lesions from deconvoluted bulk and from fourteen donors
\citep{garciaalonso2021endometrium,tan2022singlecell}, and the fifty-eight
ectopic donors pooled here could test it directly once epithelial subtypes are
resolved. Because \elaj\ retrains in hours, each new cohort can extend this
reference rather than wait on a pretraining run to be repeated. Whether the
same inductive bias scales into a pretrained model is the question these
results raise and do not answer, since bias and corpus size are independent
levers.

\elaj\ establishes a sixteen-dimensional reference for pooled endometrial
single-cell data. It prices donor removal against biology, recovers cell
identity and the cycle's compartment structure without supervision, places the
disease signal inside immune cells, and arbitrates published claims no single
cohort could settle. The
broader contribution is not the representation but the corpus made legible
through it: eleven studies that could previously only be read one at a
time.
