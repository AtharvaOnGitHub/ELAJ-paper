\section*{Discussion}

\elaj is a transformer-CVAE based disease atlas for endometriosis spanning 
eleven scRNA studies, 1{,}577{,}728 cells and 254 donors. Every donor was 
biopsied once, which created a confound between biology and batch effects 
\citep{hrovatin2025atlases}. In \elaj's sixteen-dimensional latent space,
donor identity is suppressed while cell identity and the menstrual cycle
remain recoverable. Cell identity separates without ever having been
supervised, and the cycle resolves within cell types known to interact 
with the menstrual cycle.

\elaj\ carries 1{,}883{,}986 parameters against scFoundation's 100 million, 
pretrained on over 50 million profiles \citep{hao2024scfoundation}. Four 
pretrained models conferred no advantage on this corpus over \elaj\
\citep{cui2024scgpt,theodoris2023geneformer,heimberg2025scimilarity,kedzierska2025zeroshot}.
scGPT's own authors note that its pretraining does not itself mitigate 
batch effects \citep{cui2024scgpt}. \elaj's size is what made the trade-off
measurable: eleven configurations were trained and scored on identical cells,
where a zero-shot representation from fixed pretrained weights yields a 
single point.

The \elaj frontier is defined on menstrual phase against donor or study
identity. Both are measured across donors: a cell's neighbours drawn from
other donors are asked to carry its label, and same-donor pairs are discarded
before scoring. Neither axis can therefore be won by placing each person in
their own territory \citep{squair2021confronting}. The frontier itself is
shaped by adversarial weight and information cost. While adversarial weight 
improves the achievable trade-off up to a critical value and gives worse results 
past it, the information cost slides the model along the trade-off. Integration
methods that expose one correction strength therefore report a position on
this trade-off rather than an optimum
\citep{korsunsky2019harmony,lopez2018scvi,xu2021scanvi,hie2019scanorama}.
None of the nine competing representations reached \elaj's cross-donor cycle
agreement at a lower donor recovery. Cell identity was recovered by every one
of them (Table~1), so the cycle and donor identity are the axes on which these 
representations differ \citep{luecken2022benchmarking}.

Cell composition does not differ between control and eutopic endometrium,
confirming at 194 donors a claim made in 60
\citep{duempelmann2026endometrium}. The claim that endometriosis abolishes
cyclical variation in immune composition failed at 112 and 101 donors against
the 13 that produced it \citep{huang2023immune}. The claim that prior
endometrial atlases lack reproducible marker gene signatures does not survive
either: read on a common gene space, the two cohorts here that annotated
their own cells share up to 76 of each family's top hundred genes, where a
label shuffle never shares more than three \citep{mareckova2024heca}. The disease signal that does
survive lies inside immune cells, where decoding it returned macrophage-led
inflammation. Its leading genes overlap an independent cohort's upregulated
list at \textit{P}~=~3.95~\texttimes~10\textsuperscript{--7}
\citep{zhao2017transcriptome}, and two of them are absent from the resources
that index gene-disease associations \citep{cominelli2014mmp27}. Eleven
studies had converged on overlapping findings with no means of confirming one
another, because no two share donors or a cell vocabulary
\citep{mareckova2024heca}. Confirmation, contradiction, and external
replication are three functions a disease atlas is best suited to perform.

Whether a person has endometriosis was recoverable from endometrium inside the
uterus. Severity, a grouping derived from rASRM staging, was legible only when
controls were included. A headcount of cell families did not separate severity
either. Only the separation of control from disease passed its permutation
null, indicating that rASRM staging is not recoverable from single-cell
expression.

The main architectural bet made by \elaj is the replacement of scale with
inductive bias. Ablation of pathway groups showed the biological axis
degrading, while menstrual-phase head supervision proved to be a regulariser
over the whole latent. Without the elastic cell similarity head, large
single-study territories appear that \elaj\textsubscript{B\,hi} does not
have. Replacing the dataset-native sentinel with zeros reorganises the latent
the same visible way. Removing the pooled order statistics,
which cuts what each group hands forward from 288 numbers to 32, cost the
menstrual cycle heavily and cell identity almost nothing. A weighted average
discards the shape of a group's distribution, and on this corpus that shape is
what the cycle is carried in.

Epithelial pseudotime and serum progesterone, neither of which the model was
given, were recovered more strongly than mitochondrial fraction, library size
or doublet score. The sixteen dimensions are therefore organised by biology
rather than by assay variation. What the cycle axis recovers is menstrual
physiology and not a re-expression of the label it was trained on: an axis
fitted on two cohorts ordered a third by clinical cycle day and by
circulating progesterone, resolved cycle day inside a single phase label,
and closed into a loop whose secretory half occupies five times the arc of
its proliferative half. A model told only which of nine phases a cell came
from reconstructed the shape of the cycle those phases divide.
Tissue of origin, the one label supplied through decoder conditioning, is
largely absent from the latent, which is the conditioning working as designed.
The latent keeps what was withheld and drops what was supplied. Cell-family
labels transfer across studies accurately enough to annotate the cohorts that
didn't have them, which enabled the composition tests above.

Notably, the negative results reported here are bounded by donor count rather 
than by biology, since the width of a permutation null is set by the number 
of people contributing to it. Severity and surgical stage remain claims that 
a larger corpus could settle. Three further constraints on this corpus, 
overlapping donor identifiers across one study pair, a single cohort 
recording cycle day, and an unstratified permutation null, are detailed 
in Supplementary Note~3.

Transfer beyond endometriosis rests on two conditions rather than on the
disease, scarce donors and severe batch structure. A less differentiated 
epithelium has been reported in lesions from deconvoluted bulk and from 
fourteen donors \citep{garciaalonso2021endometrium,tan2022singlecell}, and 
the fifty-eight ectopic donors pooled here could test it directly if 
epithelial subtypes are resolved. Because \elaj\ retrains in hours, each 
new cohort can extend this reference rather than wait on a pretraining run 
to be repeated. Whether the same inductive bias scales into a pretrained 
model is the question these results cannot answer, since bias and corpus 
size are independent levers. How the grouping is built is a third lever. Ours was cut
computationally from Reactome and GO with no biologist choosing which genes
belong together, so a curated partition carries more prior knowledge and
could be tested against it directly. Learning the assignment instead trades
that knowledge for flexibility, and assignment has a combinatorial number of
settings, so we would expect it to cost data and stability both. On a corpus
limited by donors, a fixed partition is the conservative choice.

\elaj\ establishes a sixteen-dimensional reference for pooled endometrial
single-cell data. It prices donor removal against biology, recovers cell
identity and the cycle's compartment structure without supervision, places the
disease signal inside immune cells, and arbitrates published claims no single
cohort could settle. The broader contribution is the corpus: eleven studies 
that could previously only be read one at a time.
