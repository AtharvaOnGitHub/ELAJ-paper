\section*{Discussion}

Eleven endometriosis studies, 1{,}577{,}728 cells and 254 donors reduce to
sixteen numbers per cell. The menstrual cycle survives in them and the donor
is hard to recover. Every donor was biopsied once, so menstrual phase is
constant within a donor and varies between them, which is the geometry of a
batch effect \citep{hrovatin2025atlases}. A correction aimed at either
reaches for the other. The supervised head supplies a third of that cycle
margin. The rest is structure: the cycle splits by cell type in the latent,
and a single linear map that was never told which cell it was reading cannot
have produced that.

Neither result required scale. \elaj\ carries 1{,}883{,}986 parameters
against scFoundation's 100 million, pretrained on over 50 million profiles
\citep{hao2024scfoundation}, and four pretrained models conferred no
advantage on this corpus
\citep{cui2024scgpt,theodoris2023geneformer,heimberg2025scimilarity,kedzierska2025zeroshot}.
Pretraining is mismatched to the question rather than worthless. Its own
authors note that scGPT's pretraining does not itself mitigate batch effects
\citep{cui2024scgpt}, and a frozen representation yields one point where this
question needs a curve. A model of this size retrains in hours, so eleven
configurations were trained and scored on identical cells. Affordable
retraining is what made the trade-off measurable at all.

A single correction strength does not describe the operation either. The
adversarial weight moved the achievable trade-off and then stopped improving
it. The information cost slid the model along that trade-off without moving
it. Two models both described as corrected can therefore occupy different
points on one curve
\citep{korsunsky2019harmony,lopez2018scvi,xu2021scanvi,hie2019scanorama}.
Stating the trade-off, measuring both axes and naming the point occupied is
the practice this corpus argues for. Held-out reconstruction is no guide to
that point, since the arm that reconstructed best held the least biology.

The instruments the field scores this operation with do not discriminate on a
corpus of this shape. Cross-donor agreement discards same-donor pairs before
scoring, so no representation can win it by giving each person their own
territory \citep{squair2021confronting}. Two standard batch metrics were
perfectly degenerate across all ten representations. The biological half saturated:
cell identity is something every method here recovers, and therefore
something that cannot rank methods \citep{luecken2022benchmarking}. Agreement
on disease status is worse than uninformative. It tracked donor recovery
across the ten while cycle agreement did not, and disease status is constant
within a donor, so a representation that remembers donors is handed that
label free. The framework is sound; it is being read outside the regime it
was built for.

What the pooled corpus settles is what its component cohorts could not.
Eleven studies have converged on overlapping findings and none can confirm
another, because no two share donors or a cell vocabulary
\citep{mareckova2024heca}. Cell composition does not differ between control
and eutopic endometrium, confirming at 194 donors a claim made in 60
\citep{duempelmann2026endometrium}. The claim that endometriosis abolishes
cyclical variation in immune composition failed at 112 and 101 donors against
the 13 that produced it \citep{huang2023immune}. The disease signal that does
survive lies inside immune cells. Decoding it returned macrophage-led
inflammation, whose leading genes overlap an independent cohort's upregulated
list at \textit{P}~=~3.95~\texttimes~10\textsuperscript{--7}
\citep{zhao2017transcriptome}, two of them absent from the resources that
index gene-disease associations \citep{cominelli2014mmp27}. Confirmation,
contradiction and external replication are three functions no cohort can
perform on itself.

Read through the latent, the clinical scale comes apart. Whether a person has
endometriosis was recoverable from endometrium inside the uterus. It was
recoverable in the representation from which donors are hardest to identify,
which makes that score the least contaminated available. Severity was legible
only through the contrast with its absence: among donors who have the disease
the surgical grades did not separate, and a headcount of cell families did
not separate them either. Revised American Society for
Reproductive Medicine staging scores what a surgeon sees at one operation,
and was built to predict fertility outcomes rather than symptoms. It is
nonetheless what cohorts record, what trials stratify on, and the number a
patient is handed to account for their pain. Diagnosis still takes 11.1 years
and ends in laparoscopy \citep{bontempo2025diagnostic}. A scale graded by
what affected tissue distinguishes is the one worth building.

Several limitations bound these interpretations. One study pair deposits the
same fifteen people under two sets of identifiers, which a donor-level split
cannot detect. So 4.3\% of held-out cells carry a same-person neighbour
scored as cross-donor. Phase and disease are donor-constant, which makes that
fraction the ceiling on the inflation. Cell families outside the
two annotated studies are themselves model predictions, which is why the
immune result is reported on annotated donors alone. Cycle-day continuity
rests on the one cohort that records it, and 2.87\% of cells came from a
follicular-fluid aspirate that entered labelled as endometrium, contributing
neither cell-family nor phase labels. The permutation null reassigns labels
across all eleven studies at once, so it never sees residual study structure.
A within-study permutation is the remedy and is not built here. Two published
claims could not be tested at all: the largest effects reported anywhere in
this corpus come from a cohort whose controls we do not hold
\citep{shih2022menstrual}, and metabolic reprogramming
\citep{sarsenova2024metabolic} needs pathway groups finer than our vocabulary.

Three questions follow. Whether this architecture scales into a pretrained
model is untested and worth testing, since the inductive bias it carries is
orthogonal to corpus size. Whether it transfers beyond endometriosis depends
on two conditions rather than on the disease, scarce donors and severe batch
structure, which hold wherever diagnosis requires surgery. And the claims
this corpus cannot yet settle, chiefly the less differentiated epithelium
reported in lesions \citep{garciaalonso2021endometrium,tan2022singlecell},
now await annotation rather than scale.

A sixteen-number cell is useful because it is a common coordinate system. A
new cohort can be placed beside the eleven here without being corrected into
them. A pattern reported in one study can be looked for in another rather
than argued about. The model is small enough that the next cohort published
does not wait on anyone repeating a pretraining run. Endometriosis research
has been held back by cohorts that cannot be read together. This is a way to
read them together.
