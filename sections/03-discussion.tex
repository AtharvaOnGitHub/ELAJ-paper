\section*{Discussion}

\elaj is a transformer-CVAE based disease atlas for endometriosis spanning 
eleven scRNA studies, 1{,}577{,}728 cells and 254 donors. Every donor was 
biopsied once, which created a confound between biology and batch effects 
\citep{hrovatin2025atlases}. \elaj's sixteen-dimensional latent space 
balances the trade-off by preserving endometriosis biology and limiting 
donor identity. Cell type clustering emerges in the latent without 
supervised training and the menstrual cycle is captured within relevant 
cell-type labels.

\elaj\ carries 1{,}883{,}986 parameters against scFoundation's 100 million, 
pretrained on over 50 million profiles \citep{hao2024scfoundation}. Four 
pretrained models conferred no advantage on this corpus over \elaj\
\citep{cui2024scgpt,theodoris2023geneformer,heimberg2025scimilarity,kedzierska2025zeroshot}.
scGPT's own authors note that its pretraining does not itself mitigate 
batch effects \citep{cui2024scgpt}. The small size allowed \elaj to iterate 
for the best solution while keeping training cost low.

The \elaj frontier, defined on menstrual phase against donor or study identity,
is shaped by adversarial weight and information cost. While adversarial weight 
improves the achievable trade-off up to a critical value and gives worse results 
past it, the information cost slides the model along the trade-off. This shows 
that a single correction strength does not optimise the trade-off as 
assumed by simpler methods \citep{korsunsky2019harmony,lopez2018scvi,xu2021scanvi,hie2019scanorama}.
Consequently, none of the nine competing representations reached
\elaj's cross-donor cycle agreement at a lower donor recovery.

Cell composition does not differ between control and eutopic endometrium,
confirming at 194 donors a claim made in 60
\citep{duempelmann2026endometrium}. The claim that endometriosis abolishes
cyclical variation in immune composition failed at 112 and 101 donors against
the 13 that produced it \citep{huang2023immune}. The disease signal that does
survive lies inside immune cells, where decoding it returned macrophage-led
inflammation. Its leading genes overlap an independent cohort's upregulated
list at \textit{P}~=~3.95~\texttimes~10\textsuperscript{--7}
\citep{zhao2017transcriptome}, and two of them are absent from the resources
that index gene-disease associations \citep{cominelli2014mmp27}. Eleven
studies had converged on overlapping findings with no means of confirming one
another, because no two share donors or a cell vocabulary
\citep{mareckova2024heca}. Confirmation, contradiction, and external
replication are three functions a disease atlas is best suited to perform.

The rASRM staging does not hold in biological context. Whether a person has
endometriosis was recoverable from endometrium inside the uterus. Severity, 
a grouping derived from rASRM staging, was not legible without control samples.
A headcount of cell families did not separate severity either. Severity was 
only separable between control and disease cells indicating that rASRM staging 
does not align with biological signals.

The main architectural bet made by \elaj is the replacement of scale with
inductive bias. Ablation of pathway groups showed the biological axis
degrading, while menstrual-phase head supervision proved to be a regulariser
over the whole latent rather than a route by which one label re-enters it.
Random groups of the same shape, parameter count and gene space gave the best
held-out reconstruction in the project and the weakest biology, because the
decoder's reliance on the latent over the covariates it is handed fell by two
thirds. It had found a cheaper route and reconstructed provenance instead of
cells. The phase head, by contrast, costs nothing in training fit and buys
both donor-blindness and study mixing at the selected checkpoint.

Two components are nearly invisible in the metrics and plain in the
embedding. Without the elastic cell similarity head, large single-study
territories appear that \elaj\textsubscript{B\,hi} does not have, and
replacing the dataset-native sentinel with zeros reorganises the latent the
same visible way, each at a cost under 1\% on every biological axis. Absence
of measurement is not a measured absence. The pooled order statistics are the
component the metrics do capture, and cutting what each group hands forward
from 288 numbers to 32 cost menstrual phase heavily while leaving cell family
untouched. What a weighted average discards is the shape of a group's
distribution, and on this corpus that shape is a cycle mechanism.

The latent holds quantities nobody supplied it. Epithelial pseudotime and
serum progesterone are recovered more strongly than mitochondrial fraction,
library size or doublet score, so biology outranks technical variation across
the sixteen dimensions. Tissue of origin is the exception that confirms the
design: it is largely absent, because the decoder was handed it and the
encoder had no reason to keep it. Cell-family labels transfer through the same
space across studies at the accuracy the composition tests required, which is
what allowed the six cohorts that deposited no annotation at all to enter
them.

Several directions follow. Whether this architecture scales into a pretrained
model is the question these results raise and do not answer, since inductive
bias and corpus size are independent levers. Transfer beyond endometriosis
rests on two conditions rather than on the disease, scarce donors and severe
batch structure, which hold wherever diagnosis requires surgery. A less
differentiated epithelium has been reported in lesions from deconvoluted bulk
and from fourteen donors
\citep{garciaalonso2021endometrium,tan2022singlecell}, and the fifty-eight
ectopic donors pooled here could test it directly once epithelial subtypes are
resolved. Because \elaj\ retrains in hours, each new cohort can extend this
reference rather than wait on a pretraining run to be repeated. We envision an
endometriosis atlas that grows with its field, where a pattern reported in one
cohort is looked for in the others rather than argued about, and where the
cost of asking is an afternoon.
