\section*{Results}

The results of this study are divided across 3 main categories. The first is 
about how \elaj performs against foundation models and other baselines. These 
represent a numerical evaluation of the model, describe the frontier, and 
outline model performance against benchmarks. The second category of results is 
understanding what \elaj itself is doing. This covers analysis of the \elaj 
latent space, ablation studies, and other ways of understanding the model itself.
The third category of results is the discoveries made and claims validated 
by \elaj. Here we test claims made by component datasets and present evidence 
for the new discoveries made from this work.


% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Batch effects and biology have a measurable trade-off}

In this corpus, each donor is sampled only once, which creates a structural 
confound between batch effects and biological signal that cannot be 
disentangled. Two training-time coefficients, the adversarial weight and the 
KL term, let \elaj{} optimise both confounding variables together, creating a 
Pareto front we call the \elaj{} frontier. Table 1 compares \elaj against 
nine other models and we find that \elaj is simultaneously better on 
preserving biological signal and removing donor identity.


Six arms spanned the two coefficients. Donor recovery ranged from 0.3975 to
0.5837 and cross-donor cycle agreement from 0.5922 to 0.7346, varying
monotonically together (Fig.~\ref{fig:knobs}a). Adversarial pressure was not
uniformly priced. Raising the weight from 7 to 168 reduced donor recovery
from 0.5837 to 0.5188 at no cost in cycle agreement, 0.7337 against 0.7346.
Raising it to 336 reversed both, to 0.5277 and 0.7146. The frontier therefore
has a knee, and the two arms reported throughout bracket it:
\elaj$_{B\,hi}$ holds cycle agreement at 0.7346, and \elaj$_{B\,lo}$ concedes
it to 0.6033 for donor recovery 0.3975.

No competing representation reached that frontier. Scored on the identical
corpus, \elaj$_{B\,hi}$ was better than all nine on both axes at once: donor
recovery 0.5188 against 0.5421 to 0.7892, and cycle agreement 0.7346 against
0.4372 to 0.7303 (Table~1). Four of the nine were pretrained foundation
models. \elaj{} carries 1,883,986 parameters and sixteen latent dimensions,
against scFoundation's 100 million parameters, 3,072 dimensions and 50
million pretraining cells \citep{hao2024scfoundation}.

Suppression was incomplete. Across the whole corpus, 32.7\% of a cell's
hundred nearest neighbours still came from its own donor, against 0.60\%
expected over 269 identifiers, an enrichment of 54.5-fold. No representation
tested retained less, and none approached donor-blindness. Across the ten,
donor recovery predicted the disease score ($\rho = +0.721$, $P = 0.019$) and
not cycle agreement ($\rho = +0.176$, $P = 0.627$). The cycle advantage was
therefore not a by-product of retained identity.

\subsection*{Sixteen dimensions carry cell identity and the menstrual
cycle}

Both labels are recovered cross-donor on all 1,577,728 cells with parameters
frozen, discarding same-donor neighbour pairs before scoring
(Methods, \emph{Whole-corpus embedding}). Cell family agreement reaches
0.9807 across 630,689 annotated cells from 118 donors ($p = 0.005$), and the
four compartments occupy contiguous, non-overlapping territory
(Fig.~\ref{fig:model}d). Cross-donor menstrual phase agreement reaches 0.6796
across 723,599 cells from 142 donors ($p = 0.005$). Phase was supplied to a
single linear classification head during training; cell family was supplied
to nothing, and emerges from reconstruction pressure and pathway grouping
alone.

Technical variation does not account for either. Under a best-of-sixteen
linear criterion, mitochondrial fraction, total counts and doublet score
reach 0.3396, 0.2514 and 0.1366. Serum progesterone reaches 0.4725 and
epithelial pseudotime 0.5801 (Supplementary Table~S1). That criterion
takes the strongest correlation available across sixteen dimensions, making
the technical values upper bounds. Biological covariates the model never
received still exceed every one of them.
