\section*{Results}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Removing the donor costs cycle signal, and two knobs trace
the trade-off}

Donor identity dominates the representation this corpus invites. Across all
1,577,728 cells, 32.7\% of a cell's hundred nearest neighbours originate from
the same donor, against a chance rate of 0.60\% computed over 269 donor
identifiers. Provenance is enriched 54.5-fold, and no biological covariate
measured here approaches that concentration. Each donor contributed a single
biopsy, so menstrual phase is constant within a donor and a per-donor offset
is geometrically indistinguishable from a batch effect. Suppression of
provenance therefore removes cycle-dependent variation alongside it, and the
quantity worth reporting is the exchange rate rather than either term alone.

Two training-time coefficients govern that suppression: the weight on the
domain-adversarial branch and the KL coefficient on the latent
(Methods, \emph{Composite objective}). Six arms span them. Donor recovery
ranges from 0.3975 to 0.5837 and cross-donor cycle agreement from 0.5922 to
0.7346, moving together monotonically (Fig.~\ref{fig:knobs}a). Adversarial
pressure is not uniformly priced. Raising the weight from 7 to 168 reduces
donor recovery from 0.5837 to 0.5188 while cycle agreement remains
indistinguishable, 0.7337 against 0.7346. Raising it to 336 recovers nothing:
donor recovery returns to 0.5277 and cycle agreement falls to 0.7146. The
adversary saturates, and beyond saturation the KL coefficient is the only
remaining lever.

Two arms are reported throughout. \elaj$_{B\,hi}$ retains cycle agreement at
0.7346 with donor recovery 0.5188; \elaj$_{B\,lo}$ suppresses study structure
substantially harder, 0.6544 against 0.7613, and concedes cycle agreement to
0.6033. Scored against nine competing representations on the identical
corpus, \elaj$_{B\,hi}$ is not merely competitive. Every one of the nine is
simultaneously worse on both axes, with higher donor recovery, 0.5421 to
0.7892, and lower cycle agreement, 0.4372 to 0.7303
(Fig.~\ref{fig:knobs}b). Four pretrained foundation models are among them.
\elaj{} carries 1,883,986 parameters across sixteen latent dimensions, against
scFoundation's 100 million across 3,072 (Fig.~\ref{fig:knobs}c).

Donor recovery is not a neutral nuisance axis. Across the ten representations
it predicts the disease score, Spearman $\rho = +0.721$ at $p = 0.019$, and
does not predict cycle agreement, $\rho = +0.176$ at $p = 0.627$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:knobs}d). Both labels are constant within a donor, so the
asymmetry is not a property of the annotation. A representation retaining
individual identity scores better on disease specifically, which makes the
disease axis a partial readout of memorisation rather than a clean
performance axis.

\subsection*{Sixteen dimensions carry cell identity and the menstrual
cycle}

Representation content was quantified on all 1,577,728 cells with parameters
frozen, so none of the labels below participated in checkpoint selection
(Methods, \emph{Whole-corpus embedding}). Agreement is cross-donor
throughout: same-donor neighbour pairs are discarded before scoring, and each
value is reported against its own permutation null rather than against an
arbitrary threshold.

Cell family agreement reaches 0.9807 across 630,689 annotated cells from 118
donors ($p = 0.005$), and the four compartments occupy contiguous,
non-overlapping territory (Fig.~\ref{fig:model}d). Cross-donor menstrual phase
agreement reaches 0.6796 across 723,599 cells from 142 donors ($p = 0.005$).
The asymmetry between those two is instructive. Phase was supplied to a single
linear classification head during training; cell family was supplied to
nothing, and is recovered from reconstruction pressure and pathway grouping
alone.

Technical variation bounds the alternative explanation rather than competing
with it. Under a best-of-sixteen linear criterion, mitochondrial fraction,
total counts and doublet score reach 0.3396, 0.2514 and 0.1366, against 0.4725
for serum progesterone and 0.5801 for epithelial pseudotime
(Supplementary Table~S1). The criterion takes the strongest correlation
available across sixteen dimensions, so these controls are upper bounds and do
not sit near zero by construction. Biological covariates the model never
received nonetheless exceed every technical one.
