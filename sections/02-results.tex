\subsection*{[4] The cycle is a cycle, and it moves only the cells it should}
\note{CARRIES C2, C3. FIGURE 2. THE HEADLINE.
\\C2, AND THE ARGUMENT MATTERS MORE THAN THE NUMBERS: displacement in units
of each cell type's own scatter --- stromal 1.07, epithelial 0.98,
endothelial 0.39, immune 0.34, identical across both frontier arms. The
phase head is a single linear map over the whole latent and was never given
cell type, so it cannot have produced that split. Claim the SPLIT, not the
signal: the ablation shows the head supplies about 69.5\% of the phase
margin, so ``the cycle was recovered'' is not available, but ``the
cell-type structure of the cycle was recovered'' is.
\\C3: day 8 through 15--20 to 25--30 with the ends meeting, read off a
cyclic colour map rather than inferred.
\\HONEST LIMIT IN THE SAME BREATH, not a footnote: the day values come from
one cohort, 60 donors, and every one of them contributes exactly one day.
\\\textcolor{elajcoral}{DO NOT CLAIM donors are spread across the
embedding. They are not. Three donors at days 6, 12 and 29 occupy visibly
different regions, and donor identity is 14$\times$ enriched among a cell's
thirty nearest neighbours against chance. That is the confound showing in
our own latent, and it is the reason the phase number discards same-donor
neighbours. If a panel shows donor colour, it argues for the estimator, not
for the result.}
\\$\rightarrow$ hands on: time is in there. What about the disease?}

\subsection*{[6] What the latent needs in order to work}
\note{CARRIES D1--D6, B2, B3. FIGURE 5.
\\AW: ``these are the results that a true ML researcher would actually want
to read properly. The fact that we have them and we want to publish them is
itself a huge thing for a paper like ours to do.'' So this subsection is
written for that reader and is not compressed to make room elsewhere.
\\FIVE SINGLE-KEY ABLATIONS against one base, predictions registered in each
config before the runs started, 6 of 11 bands landed, misses reported.
\\D1 FIRST AND MOST PROMINENTLY, because every reported arm is
semi-supervised and a reviewer will ask whether the cycle result is the
supervision played back: removing the phase head drops cross-donor phase
0.7346 to 0.4812 against chance 0.370. The head supplies about 69.5\% of the
margin, the architecture 30.5\%.
\\D2, THE SURPRISE: the same head is a regulariser. Without it
\texttt{train\_nb} falls 0.4140 to 0.3772 while \texttt{val\_nb} rises
0.4083 to 0.4256, diverging from epoch 1.
\\D3: ECS traverses the frontier rather than expanding it --- removing it
improves every biology axis and worsens every cost axis, which makes it the
same kind of knob as \texttt{w\_kl\_gauss} rather than an independent gain.
Best shown by plotting it ON Figure 1b's frontier.
\\D4 statistics are a phase mechanism (phase $-0.096$, family $-0.002$).
D5 the sentinel is load-bearing for study mixing (0.6043 to 0.6260).
D6 random grouping costs disease most, $-20\%$, in the arm with the best
reconstruction in the project.
\\B2 HERE RATHER THAN WITH THE FRONTIER, per AW --- it is something we found
out about the model, not something the model found out about the data: a
discriminator driven to chance is not evidence that the latent is
study-free. Adversary loss correlated $+0.883$ with donor kNN at m3 and
$+0.890$ with study kNN at m2\_cls40; both should be negative if the term
worked. B3: discriminator capacity was the cause, not batch composition.
\\\emph{Adversarial study removal head} in Methods points forward to this.
If B2 is cut, that pointer goes with it.}
