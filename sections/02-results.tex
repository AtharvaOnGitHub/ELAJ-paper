\section*{Results}

The results of this study are divided across 3 main categories. The first is 
about how \elaj performs against foundation models and other baselines. These 
represent a numerical evaluation of the model, describe the frontier, and 
outline model performance against benchmarks. The second category of results is 
understanding what \elaj itself is doing. This covers analysis of the \elaj 
latent space, ablation studies, and other ways of understanding the model itself.
The third category of results is the discoveries made and claims validated 
by \elaj. Here we test claims made by component datasets and present evidence 
for the new discoveries made from this work.


% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{Batch effects and biology have a measurable trade-off}

In this corpus, each donor is sampled only once, which creates a structural 
confound between batch effects and biological signal that cannot be 
disentangled. Two training-time coefficients, the adversarial weight and the 
KL term, let \elaj{} optimise both confounding variables together, creating a 
Pareto front we call the \elaj{} frontier. Table 1 compares \elaj against 
nine other models and we find that \elaj is simultaneously better on 
preserving biological signal and removing donor identity.

Donor recovery and cycle agreement degraded together across the six arms,
spanning 0.3975 to 0.5837 and 0.5922 to 0.7346 respectively
(Fig.~\ref{fig:knobs}a). The exchange rate between them was not constant.
Raising the adversarial weight from 7 to 168 bought a substantial reduction
in donor recovery, 0.5837 to 0.5188, at no measurable cost in cycle
agreement. Raising it to 336 was wasteful and worsened both. Adversarial
pressure is therefore cheap until it saturates, and the two reported arms
bracket the point at which it does.

No competing representation reached that frontier. Scored on the identical
corpus, \elaj$_{B\,hi}$ was better than all nine on both axes at once, with
donor recovery 0.5188 against 0.5421 to 0.7892 and cycle agreement 0.7346
against 0.4372 to 0.7303 (Table~1). Four of the nine were pretrained
foundation models, the largest carrying 100 million parameters and 50 million
pretraining cells \citep{hao2024scfoundation} against \elaj{}'s 1,883,986 
parameters and zero pretraining. Pretraining scale conferred no advantage 
on this corpus.

\subsection*{Biology is an emergent property of training}

\elaj{} sees only gene expression, read in forty-four Reactome and GO
pathway groups, with study, tissue and platform supplied to the decoder as
covariates. Its primary objective is reconstruction but it is 
semi-supervised on menstrual phase. Cell type, disease status, surgical stage, 
severity and grade are withheld entirely (Methods, \emph{Composite objective}). 
Emergence of any of those is therefore a result of training and each is tested on
the trained model across all 1,577,728 cells, discarding same-donor
neighbours so that no label can be recovered trivially (Methods, 
\emph{Cross-donor agreement}).

Supervision accounts for a minority of the cycle signal. Removing the phase
head and retraining left cross-donor cycle agreement at 0.4812 against
0.7346, so 65.5\% of the chance-corrected signal survived the loss of the
only biological label the model was given (Table~1). What remains can be 
attributed to reconstruction through pathway groups instead of the classifier.

The cycle's cellular structure was not supplied. Displacement from
proliferative to secretory phase, scaled by each compartment's own spread,
reached 1.0677 in stromal cells and 0.9824 in epithelial cells, against
0.3918 in endothelial and 0.3409 in immune cells. The phase head is a single
linear map over the whole latent and received no cell-type information, so
that threefold ordering is an emergent property. It is also the
correct ordering, since the endometrial cycle acts principally on the
stromal and epithelial compartments.

Labels withheld from training entirely were recoverable on the frozen model.
Cell family reached 0.9807 across 630,689 annotated cells and surgical
stage 0.3320, both at $P = 0.005$ against their own permutation nulls, and
merged severity 0.1286 at $P = 0.025$. Surgical grade did not, at 0.0263 and $P = 0.408$. That 
these labels are legible at all is what makes the disease analyses below 
interpretable.

Two controls bound the alternative explanations. Under a best-of-sixteen
linear criterion the strongest technical correlations, mitochondrial fraction
at 0.3396 and genes detected at 0.3366, remained below serum progesterone at
0.4725 and epithelial pseudotime at 0.5801, neither of which the model
received. Replacing the forty-four Reactome and GO groups with random groups of
identical size then degraded every biological axis: cell family by 2.2\%,
menstrual phase by 7.7\% and disease by 19.8\%. Biological structure in the
input is therefore a cause of the biology in the latent rather than an
interpretation placed on it.

\subsection*{Endometriosis severity is not biologically rooted}

Severity in endometriosis is assigned through surgery rather than measured. The
revised American Society for Reproductive Medicine (rASRM) system scores lesion
count, depth and adhesion into stages I to IV; \texttt{duempelmann\_2026}
grades its patients mild or severe and \texttt{marecko\_2024} records rASRM
stage (Methods, \emph{Severity}). Neither was given to the model.

Disease status is legible and severity is not. Cross-donor agreement
separating control from eutopic endometrium reached 0.3364 at $P = 0.005$.
Restricted to patients, mild against severe reached 0.0744 and failed its
permutation null at $P = 0.129$ over 57 donors; surgical grade failed at
0.0263 and $P = 0.408$ over 60, and was negative in all six frontier arms.
The only contrasts that cleared their nulls were those
containing controls: merged severity at 0.1286 and rASRM stage at 0.3320,
the latter with 36 of its 58 donors at stage 0. Nor is the failure peculiar
to \elaj{}. Scored on the same held-out donors, severity among patients was
negative in all ten representations, between $-$0.0090 and $-$0.1592, and
not one cleared its permutation null ($P \geq 0.627$). Restoring the
controls turns every one of the ten positive.

Cell composition does not track severity either. Across fifty-one patients
the share of each family was unchanged between low and high severity, at
$P = 0.2092$ for immune cells, 0.1624 endothelial, 0.9696 stromal and 0.9130
epithelial (Fig.~X). The two readouts are independent, one a geometry in the
latent and the other a count over transferred labels, and both fail on the
same contrast. The latent separates people who have endometriosis from
people who do not, but does not separate stages of endometriosis.
