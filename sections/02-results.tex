\section*{Results}

The results of this study are divided across 2 main categories. The first is 
about how \elaj performs against foundation models and other baselines. These 
represent a numerical evaluation of the model, describe the frontier, and 
outline model performance against benchmarks. The second category of results is 
understanding what \elaj itself is doing. This covers analysis of the \elaj 
latent space, ablation studies, and other ways of understanding the model itself.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{[1] The \elaj frontier}

Six models were trained, varying the adversarial weight over three values at
two settings of the latent's information cost. The best model was chosen on 
reconstruction loss value on the validation set. (Fig.~1b, c).

At the lower information cost, raising the adversarial weight from the lowest
to the middle value reduced donor recovery from 0.5837 to 0.5188 and study
mixing from 0.7183 to 0.6043, while cross-donor phase agreement was 0.7346
against 0.7337 and cell family 0.9820 against 0.9829. Raising it to the
highest value gave donor recovery 0.5277, study mixing 0.6530 and phase
0.7146.

Raising the information cost reduced donor recovery and study mixing at every
adversarial weight, to a minimum of 0.3975 and 0.5129, and reduced
cross-donor phase agreement to between 0.5922 and 0.6791 and cell family to
between 0.9553 and 0.9841.

All results below are reported from the model at the middle adversarial
weight and the lower information cost: cross-donor phase agreement 0.7346
against a chance rate of 0.370, cell family 0.9820, donor recovery 0.5188 and
study mixing 0.6043.

\subsection*{[3] The cycle is a cycle, and it moves only the cells it should}

The decoder is conditioned on study, platform and tissue. Cycle day, serum
progesterone, cell type and disease status were not inputs. Menstrual phase
was supervised, through a single linear map over the whole sixteen-dimensional
latent.

Cross-donor phase agreement on held-out donors was 0.7346 against a chance
rate of 0.370, over 195{,}879 cells, at the smallest attainable permutation
$p$ of 0.005. Over all 1{,}577{,}728 cells it was 0.6796, across 142 donors
and 723{,}599 cells, at the same $p$.

Displacement between the proliferative and secretory centroids, expressed in
units of each cell type's own scatter, was 1.07 for stromal cells, 0.98 for
epithelial, 0.39 for endothelial and 0.34 for immune. In the arm at the higher
information cost the same four quantities were 1.07, 0.99, 0.54 and 0.29. The
ordering was the same in both; the two smaller values were not (Fig.~2a, b).

Removing the phase head reduced cross-donor phase agreement from 0.7346 to
0.4812, leaving 30.5\% of the margin over chance.

Two continuous quantities that were not model inputs were correlated against
the latent, taking the largest absolute correlation over its sixteen
dimensions: 0.58 for epithelial pseudotime and 0.47 for serum progesterone.
The same procedure applied to four quality-control measures gave 0.34 for
mitochondrial fraction, 0.34 for genes detected, 0.25 for total counts and
0.14 for doublet score.

Cycle day was recorded for 466{,}176 cells, 29.6\% of the corpus, in one
cohort of 60 donors. Each donor contributed a single day, between day 5 and
day 30, with 22 of those 26 days represented. Colouring those cells by day and
advancing one day at a time moved the dense region of the embedding
progressively and returned it to its starting position (Fig.~2c--e).

Among a cell's hundred nearest neighbours, 0.327 shared its donor, against a
chance rate of 0.006.

\subsection*{[4] Disease signal, and where it is not}

Within each study and cell type separately, the distance between the case and
control centroids was measured in units of that stratum's own scatter. In
marecko\_2024 the largest value was the immune compartment, at 0.77, against
0.61 for epithelial, 0.58 for endothelial and 0.43 for stromal cells. In the
arm at the higher information cost the immune compartment was again the
largest, at 0.83 (Fig.~4a).

For scale, the separations between whole-corpus cell-family centroids,
measured the same way, run from 0.76 for epithelial against stromal cells to
1.53 for endothelial against immune.

In duempelmann\_2026 the same four strata gave 0.23, 0.21, 0.29 and 0.21, and
in the second arm 0.27, 0.25, 0.19 and 0.22. The largest value was the immune
compartment in the first arm and the endothelial compartment in the second
(Fig.~4b).

Cross-donor agreement on disease status, which discards same-donor neighbours
and is not the same measurement, was 0.3364 pooled across studies on 172{,}740
held-out cells, and 0.135, 0.064 and 0.000 within marecko\_2024,
duempelmann\_2026 and lin\_2023 taken separately. Across all 1{,}577{,}728
cells, agreement on disease severity in each study's own vocabulary was 0.2655
over 118 donors and 67 label levels, at a permutation $p$ of 0.005.

Surgical stage was recorded for 58 donors in a single cohort. Cross-donor
agreement on it was $+$0.1313 on held-out donors at $p = 0.3035$, and $+$0.332
across the whole corpus at $p = 0.005$. Surgical grade was recorded for 60
donors in a single, different cohort; cross-donor agreement was $-$0.0414 on
held-out donors at $p = 0.4975$, and $+$0.0263 across the whole corpus at
$p = 0.408$. Across the six ablated configurations, held-out stage ranged from
$+$0.0799 to $+$0.2190 at $p$ between 0.1194 and 0.3284, and held-out grade was
negative in every one, between $-$0.0227 and $-$0.0723, at $p$ between 0.4378
and 0.6269. The smallest attainable $p$ on this test is 0.005, which cell
family and menstrual phase both reach.
