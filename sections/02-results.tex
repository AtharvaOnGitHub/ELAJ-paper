Five models were trained, each removing exactly one component from
\elaj{} and changing nothing else: the menstrual-phase head, the elastic
cell similarity term, the order statistics in the group readout, the
sentinel marking unmeasured genes, and the pathway grouping, replaced by
a random partition of the same group sizes. All five completed, and all
five hold 1{,}883{,}986 parameters --- the pooled statistics are computed
rather than learned, so removing them frees none. Each was scored exactly
as the reported model was, and each is read against that model rather
than against the others.

\paragraph{The phase head supplies most of the phase signal, and not all
of it.}
Cross-donor phase agreement falls from 0.735 to 0.481 when the head is
removed, against a chance level of 0.370. The head therefore accounts for
roughly seventy per cent of the margin over chance and the architecture
for the remaining thirty. Cell family agreement moves by 0.003 over the
same change, so the model is otherwise intact. The claim this supports is
neither that menstrual phase emerges from the architecture nor that it is
simply supervised: a linear head trained on 44\% of cells supplies about
seventy per cent of the signal, and what remains is carried by a model
that was never told which cells those were.

Removing the head also removes the only thing holding the model's
generalisation together. Training reconstruction continues to improve
while validation reconstruction worsens from the first epoch onward, and
the adversarial classifier reads held-out studies increasingly well over
the same span, so the reported checkpoint is the first epoch. The head is
acting as a regulariser as much as a supervisor.

\paragraph{Elastic cell similarity moves along the frontier rather than
extending it.}
Removing it improves every biological axis --- cell family, phase and
disease --- and worsens every cost axis, with study mixing rising from
0.604 to 0.683 and donor identifiability from 0.519 to 0.552. A term
carrying the largest weight in the objective might be expected to buy
biological structure; it does not. What it buys is study mixing, paid for
in biology, which makes it the same kind of control as the weight on the
latent's information cost rather than an independent improvement.

\paragraph{The order statistics carry menstrual phase.}
Replacing the nine summary tokens a group hands on with the learned token
alone costs 0.096 of cross-donor phase agreement while moving cell family
by 0.002. Phase is a property of the shape of a pathway's expression
distribution, which a weighted average discards and order statistics
retain; cell type is not, and does not move.

\paragraph{The sentinel keeps study identity out of the latent.}
Without it, study mixing rises from 0.604 to 0.626 and study purity from
0.640 to 0.660: the study becomes easier to recover from the latent.
A gene a study never measured, recorded as a zero, is zero in every cell
that study contributed, and a model given no way to tell that constant
from a measured silence can learn it as though it were biology. The
sentinel does not conceal which genes a study assayed --- it is an
explicit mark, and if anything makes the panel more legible. What it does
is deny that panel a route into the latent, because sentinel positions
are excluded from the reconstruction loss and zeros are not.

\paragraph{The pathway grouping costs reconstruction and buys biology.}
Genes reassigned at random to groups of identical sizes, leaving
parameter count, attention cost and every other setting untouched,
produced the best reconstruction of any model trained in this project,
over the longest run and with the most stable training curves: training
and validation descending together and converging smoothly. Judged on its
optimisation the model had converged well. Judged on its latent it had
not. Cross-donor cell family agreement fell from 0.982 to 0.961 and
cross-donor disease agreement from 0.336 to 0.270, the largest biological
loss of any arm on any axis.

The partition drawn from Reactome and Gene Ontology therefore makes
reconstruction harder and the latent more biological, and the two move in
opposite directions within a single run. The quantity the model optimises
is not the quantity the model is for, and these two numbers are the
clearest statement of that this work can make.

\paragraph{Disease stage and grade carry no recoverable signal.}
No arm, including the reported model, reaches significance on either
label: stage ranges from 0.080 to 0.219 at $p$ between 0.12 and 0.33, and
grade is negative in every configuration. This is a property of the
labels rather than a result of any ablation, and it bounds what the
latent can be asked about severity.
