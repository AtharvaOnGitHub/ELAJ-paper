\section*{Results}

The results of this study are divided across 2 main categories. The first is 
about how \elaj performs against foundation models and other baselines. These 
represent a numerical evaluation of the model, describe the frontier, and 
outline model performance against benchmarks. The second category of results is 
understanding what \elaj itself is doing. This covers analysis of the \elaj 
latent space, ablation studies, and other ways of understanding the model itself.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection*{[1] The \elaj frontier}

Six models were trained and each was selected on reconstruction loss
on the validation split alone. The models trace a Pareto front on a plot of 
menstrual phase against study separation or donor recovery. 

The models cross three adversarial weights with two settings of the latent's 
information cost. The two knobs do different things. Raising the
information cost from 1 to 30 reduces cross-donor phase agreement and improves
donor and study removal, and it does so at all three adversarial weights. At
the middle weight, where both reported arms sit, phase falls from 0.7346 to
0.6033, donor recovery from 0.5188 to 0.3975, and study separation from
0.7613 to 0.6544. So information cost travels along the Pareto front.

On the other hand, raising the adversarial weight from 7 to 168 moves the 
Pareto front itself. At lower information cost, study separation
falls from 0.8636 to 0.7613 and donor recovery from 0.5837 to 0.5188, while
cross-donor phase agreement barely changes, from 0.7337 to 0.7346. Raising it
further to 336 is worse on all three axes at both information costs (Fig.~2).

Placed on the same axes as the field, \elaj creates the frontier. Cross-donor
phase agreement of 0.7346 is the highest of the fifteen representations
measured, above scanorama's 0.7303, and donor recovery of 0.5188 is lower
than every one of the nine competing methods, the nearest being Geneformer at
0.5421. No other method is simultaneously better on both. 

Only one method competes on study removal. Harmony reaches 0.6697, between 
ELAJ$_{B\,lo}$ at 0.6544 and ELAJ$_{B\,hi}$ at 0.7613, and it pays for it in 
cycle signal: fitted transductively on the same cells as principal components, 
it cuts study separation by 30.3\% and cross-donor phase agreement by 8.2\%. 
The trade is demonstrated by the field's own correction tool.

Cell type does not separate these methods. Cross-donor agreement on cell
family runs from 0.9023 to 0.9896 across all fifteen, a spread of 0.0873,
so a representation can be judged on it only in the sense that all of them
pass. Scale does not help either: across the ten methods, the width of the
representation is negatively associated with cross-donor phase agreement
($\rho=-0.6503$, $p=0.0418$), and the two widest, scFoundation at 3{,}072
dimensions and scGPT at 512, reach 0.6449 and 0.4869 against \elaj's sixteen
dimensions.

Two arms are carried forward. ELAJ$_{B\,hi}$, at the middle adversarial
weight and the lower information cost, holds the most cycle signal: phase
0.7346, cell family 0.9820, donor recovery 0.5188, study separation 0.7613.
ELAJ$_{B\,lo}$, at the same adversarial weight and the higher information
cost, gives up cycle signal and removes more of both confounds: phase 0.6033,
cell family 0.9565, donor recovery 0.3975, study separation 0.6544.

\subsection*{[2] Reading 1.58 million cells as sixteen numbers}

The model was given expression, with study, platform and anatomical site as
decoder covariates and menstrual phase through a single supervised head. Cell
family, cell type, cycle day, serum progesterone, donor identity, disease
status, surgical stage and severity reached neither the encoder nor the
decoder. Once training ended and the checkpoint was fixed, all 1{,}577{,}728
cells were embedded and read together. Menstrual phase, the supervised
exception, is reported on held-out donors throughout while others are 
interpreted on the whole corpus.

Of the withheld variables, cell identity is the one the latent holds most
completely. Agreement among a cell's thirty nearest neighbours was 0.9975 
on a four-way lineage label over 174{,}848 cells, and 0.8316 on a 
forty-five-way cell type over 220{,}160 cells. Measured across donors, 
agreement on harmonised cell family was 0.9807 across 118 donors
($p=0.005$), the highest of any label measured here. It is clear that the 
sixteen dimensions group cells largely by cell type (Fig.~4a). It is 
relevant to state that cell type classification is unique to endometrial 
cells and cells from other anatomical sites are not labelled. (Fig.~4c) 
hints at what the cells from other anatomical sites could be.

Donor identity is the withheld variable the model is trained to discard
rather than to hold. On the held-out set, \elaj scored 0.5188 against a 
range of 0.5421 to 0.7892 for other models. As seen in the previous
section, \elaj is the best at retaining biological information while 
removing donor identity.

The next few sections use the whole corpus, not just the held-out set, to 
interpret \elaj. These interpretations are underpinned by \elaj's donor 
identity score on the full corpus: 0.3229. The low donor identity makes 
the results on other variables more donor-blind than the number on the 
held-out set would suggest.

\subsection*{[3] The menstrual cycle through \elaj}

The decoder is conditioned on study, platform and tissue alone. Menstrual phase 
was the only supervised label, provided to the latent through a single linear 
map over all sixteen dimensions. Other related metadata variables such as cycle 
day, serum progesterone, cell type and disease status were never provided as 
inputs to the model.

Cycle day, recorded for 466{,}176 cells in only one cohort of 60 donors 
representing 22 days between day 5 and 30, showed up in the latent space. 
Advancing through the days one at a time showed a dense mass of cells being 
progressively arranged in the latent space. As the cycle returned to where it 
began, so did that region, showing the cycle to be closed in the latent space 
(Fig.~5a).

Cross-donor agreement, defined in Methods, is the share of a cell's hundred
nearest latent neighbours that carry its label, counting only the neighbours
belonging to other donors. It is reported as excess over chance such that zero 
is the agreement the label's frequencies alone would produce and one is perfect. 
Significance is assessed by reassigning the same labels to different donors two 
hundred times and recomputing \cite{newman2003mixing,anderson2001permanova}, 
the donor being the unit that can be exchanged when a label is constant within 
one \cite{squair2021confronting}. On held-out donors, agreement on menstrual 
phase was 0.7346 across 36 donors ($n_{\text{cells}}=195{,}879$), and no 
permutation reached it ($p=0.005$, the smallest value two hundred permutations 
can return). It remained high when all 1{,}577{,}728 cells were read together, 
at 0.6796 across 142 donors ($p=0.005$). 

Removing the phase head reduced that agreement to 0.4812, so just under
two-thirds of the signal survived the loss of supervision (65.5\%) and the
head accounted for the remaining third.

Epithelial pseudotime and serum progesterone correlated highly (0.58 and 0.47, 
respectively) with the single best-matched of the sixteen
dimensions of the \elaj latent space. This was 
calibrated against five quality-control measures with a range of correlation
between 0.14 and 0.34, the highest being the fraction of mitochondrial reads.

\subsection*{[4] Surgical stage and grade}

Endometriosis severity is recorded across studies as surgical stage or 
endometriosis grade. It appears as rASRM I--IV in one cohort and mild against 
severe in the other, where mild and severe are defined as rASRM I--II and 
III--IV respectively. Merged onto one three-level scale (control, mild, severe) 
it covers 34 held-out donors and 118 total donors across the whole corpus.

On the 34 held-out donors, ELAJ$_{B\,hi}$ reached 0.1157 on the merged scale
and did not clear its permutation null ($p=0.289$), as did ELAJ$_{B\,lo}$ at
0.1155 ($p=0.249$). Read across the whole
corpus, agreement on it was 0.1286 ($p=0.025$) on ELAJ$_{B\,hi}$, and 0.1114
($p=0.020$) on ELAJ$_{B\,lo}$. While \elaj is
able to identify severity with some confidence, further analysis revealed that 
\elaj, being the best in the group, was only able to separate control from 
disease and not the severity reported between disease cases. With 57 donors 
having some severity of endometriosis, the best model (ELAJ$_{B\,hi}$) scored
0.0744 and could not clear its permutation null at $p=0.129$. Meanwhile,
disease status on its own, control endometrium against eutopic endometrium,
reached 0.3364 comfortably clearing the permutation null with $p=0.005$.

The field corroborates this result. No model is able to reliably interpret 
severity from biological signal alone. On held-out donors, agreement on the merged
scale ran from 0.0759 to 0.1860 across the ten methods with the lowest $p$ 
being 0.080, and not one of them reached its own permutation null. The
highest was Geneformer at 0.1860 ($p=0.080$), pretrained on corpora far
larger than this one; the lowest was scANVI at 0.0759 ($p=0.418$). Scanorama,
the method from which a donor is most easily identified in this corpus
(0.7892), reached 0.1505 ($p=0.219$), and scFoundation, the widest
representation tested at a 3{,}072-dimensional latent space, reached 0.1695
($p=0.139$). Testing the severity separability without control samples,
every one of the ten fell at or below zero, with the lowest $p$ at 0.627.

This indicates that whether a person has endometriosis is legible in their 
endometrium but the severity, even abstracted to two levels, cannot be
interpreted from biology. We read this result against other variables measured 
on the same cells: cell family reached 0.9820 and menstrual phase reached 
0.7346 in ELAJ$_{B\,hi}$, with no permutation matching either of them
($p=0.005$; Fig.~4h, 4i).

\subsection*{[5] What the disease axis measures}

Endometriosis is diagnosed by surgical visualisation of ectopic lesions,
endometrium-like tissue growing outside the uterus, and those lesions are not
sampled here. Every cell in this test is endometrium in its normal location,
eutopic, so the label separates donors whose lesions lie elsewhere from donors
with none. In the \elaj latent, cross-donor agreement
on it was 0.3364 across 36 held-out donors
($n_{\text{cells}}=172{,}740$; $p=0.005$). This was the lowest of the ten
methods tested. The highest was scanorama at 0.4290, on the same donors.

Across those ten methods, agreement on disease status tracked donor recovery
($\rho=+0.721$; $p=0.019$). Menstrual phase, measured on the same embeddings
and the same cells, did not ($\rho=+0.176$; $p=0.63$). \elaj was both the
method from which a donor was hardest to identify, at 0.5188, and the method
with the lowest agreement on disease status.

Cell family and menstrual phase, measured under the same estimator on the same
cells, both reached the permutation floor of 0.005 (Fig.~4a, 4d).
