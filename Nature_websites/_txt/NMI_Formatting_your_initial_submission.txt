Formatting your initial submission
Your initial submission does not need to be specially formatted,
as long as the study is described in a way that is suitable for
editorial assessment and peer review. 
We accept initial submissions in PDF, Word or TeX/LaTeX
formats; if you are using TeX/LaTeX, please submit compiled
PDFs. 
Please note, further formatting of all text and images will be
required if your manuscript is accepted for publication – see
formatting requirements.
