## Introduction

**Paragraph 2, everything before its last sentence** (guide: rephrase)

*Was:* "Single-cell transcriptomics is the resolution at which that biology is legible, because endometriosis is a change in what particular cells do rather than in which organ is affected. Lesions carry transcriptional programmes of their own [3,4]. The unaffected endometrium of an affected person is already altered, so the disease is not confined to the lesion [5,6]. Immune involvement recurs across every study that looks for it [7,8], and reference atlases now describe the cell types of healthy endometrium across the cycle [9]."

*Now:* "Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA) suits this question for three reasons. Unlike bulk sequencing, which averages expression over every cell in a sample, it resolves the individual cell populations that the disease alters [Haque 2017]. Unlike spatial transcriptomics, which remains costlier and lower in throughput [Moses 2022], it has been applied widely enough in endometriosis for independent cohorts to be pooled. And it is less exposed than the metabolome, which shifts with diet and time of day [Dallmann 2012], to the circumstances of a single sampling day. scRNA studies find that lesions carry transcriptional programmes of their own [3,4] and that the endometrium of an affected person is already altered [5,6]. Immune involvement recurs across these studies [7,8], and reference atlases now map the cell types of healthy endometrium across the cycle [9]."

Last sentence unchanged except "single-cell" hyphenated.
