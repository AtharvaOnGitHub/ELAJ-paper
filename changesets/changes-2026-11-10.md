**About ELAJ** **a**, The architecture, in five stages. A
cell enters as the 30,962 genes shared across the corpus and is reduced to
the 15,496 that map to a pathway group, with genes a study never assayed
carried by a did-not-sample sentinel rather than by a zero. The gene encoder
reads those genes in 44 pathway groups, normalises each group per study, and
summarises it with its own transformer into nine tokens of 32 dimensions. The
cell encoder compresses the resulting 396 tokens to sixteen. Four heads read
that latent: the study adversary (DAB), whose gradient is reversed on the way
back to the encoder; reconstruction; elastic cell similarity (ECS), which
holds similar cells together; and the supervised menstrual-phase classifier
(Phase CLS). Study, platform and anatomical site condition the reconstruction
head alone and never reach the encoder. Disease status is given to nothing.
**b**, **c**, Cross-donor
agreement on menstrual phase against each of the two costs, over all ten
representations on the same held-out donors, with the Pareto front drawn.
Higher is better vertically and lower is better horizontally, so the upper
left corner is the goal. **d**, **e**, The ELAJ B hi latent
over all 1,577,728 cells, 400,000 drawn at random, coloured by cell
family and by study. Cells with no cell-family label are grey; every cell
carries a study. Studies are named by the first author of the paper the data
come from and ordered by the number of cells they contribute.


Two readings. In **b** the front is two points and both are ours:
ELAJ B hi holds the most cycle signal of anything tested (0.7346) while
giving up the least donor identity (0.5188), and the nearest competitor on
cycle, Scanorama at 0.7303, is also the representation from which a donor is
most easily recovered (0.7892). In **c** Harmony joins the front, and
the comparison between **d** and **e** is what that costs: cell
families occupy contiguous territory while studies do not separate into
their own.
